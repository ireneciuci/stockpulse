Definition

What is the News Score?

A number from 0 to 100 that tells you whether the news about a company over the last 14 days has been good or bad for its shareholders. 50 means neutral: no news, or good and bad news that cancel out.

It measures the news, not the stock. A company can have a high score while its share price falls, and the other way round.

0–25Strongly negative
25–40Negative
40–60Mixed / neutral
60–75Positive
75–100Strongly positive
Method

How it's calculated

four steps, every 30 minutes

01
Collect.

Headlines for each company, SEC 8-K filings (earnings, executive changes, big deals) and daily prices.

02
Read.

AI checks that each item is really about the company, rates it from −1 (very bad) to +1 (very good), and its importance from 1 to 5.

03
Weigh.

Important, recent news from official sources counts most. A 3-day-old story counts half as much as today's; opinion pieces count less.

04
Score.

Everything is combined into one number from 0 to 100. With only a few articles, the score stays close to 50.
